Diversity & Inclusion

A team on and off the pitch means a team for everyone.

Our Commitment

Rugby is for everyone

The Kats have always been a club-first club — and that means making sure everyone who wants to be part of it can be, regardless of background, identity, or circumstance. Safe sport and inclusion aren't an add-on for us, they're part of how we run the club.

Safe Sport & Code of Conduct

The Kats follow BC Rugby's Safe Sport policies and adhere to the BCRU Code of Conduct in full. That means every member of our club — players, coaches, and volunteers alike — is expected to treat teammates, opponents, officials, and the wider rugby community with respect, both on and off the pitch.

If you ever have a concern about conduct, safety, or how you've been treated at the club, please reach out to team management directly, or use our contact form — concerns raised in confidence will be treated that way.

Financial Barriers

Cost should never be the reason someone can't play. If yearly dues or registration fees are a barrier for you or your family, please talk to team management — we're glad to work out assistance or alternative arrangements on a confidential, case-by-case basis.

Accommodation & Access

Building a club everyone can belong to

We actively identify and create accommodation opportunities so that members of the following communities can fully take part in the club:

Members with Disabilities

We work with players to find the accommodations that let them train, travel, and take part on matchday.

Indigenous Members

We welcome and support Indigenous players and families as part of the Kats community.

2SLGBTQIA+ Members

Every player deserves a changing room and a clubhouse where they can be themselves. That's the standard we hold ourselves to.

Newcomers to Canada

New to the country, new to rugby, or both — we'll help you find your footing on and off the pitch.

Racialized Communities

The Kats is a club for players of every background, and we work to make sure that's true in practice, not just in words.

Have a question, or need an accommodation?

If there's anything — big or small — that would help you join or stay involved with the Kats, tell us. Team management is always happy to talk through what would work for you.
